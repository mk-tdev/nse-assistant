# Agent Trading: separate prospective paper portfolio

`/agent-trading` and read-only `/api/agent-trading?session=YYYY-MM-DD` show an independent long-only paper book. Baseline collectors, scanners, live strategy controls and backtest ledgers continue unchanged. The agent book is deliberately excluded from the existing baseline positions and overview pages; its own page contains its results and costs.

Limits are enforced against actual persisted fills: ten shares, ten open positions, twenty entered stocks per IST session, one position per stock/session. Approval is not a fill. Entry requires a strictly later fresh post-approval quote within sixty seconds and the existing price/extension bounds. Paper stops/2R targets and 15:10 planned exits are managed by code even when the model, news or budget is unavailable. Genuine missing observation paths remain unknown. There are no broker order tools or Dhan credentials in the agent container.

The decision packet uses immutable candidate events from baseline/scanner strategy signals, their supplied setup/price/volume evidence, current Nifty-constituent breadth and sector proxies, and timestamped global/company headlines. It is not an official index quote or a global index price service. RSS sources are NSE announcements, BBC Business and CNBC markets; no paid news service is added. Company news coverage is partial. Unavailable required company/global sources, insufficient fresh equity breadth, expired opinions, failed validation and exhausted shared budget block new entries. WAIT candidates may be reviewed at most three times, five minutes apart, while the technical candidate is at most ten minutes old.

The model returns exactly one approve/wait/skip per supplied candidate, priority, reasons, risks and evidence IDs. Market and original candidate evidence are mandatory. News text is untrusted data. Only IDs present in the bounded packet may be cited; model-supplied prices are not accepted. Decisions and full point-in-time request packets remain in SQLite, including publication/first-seen news timestamps, validation failures, model usage and reservation records. Historical views never attach current headlines to old entry decisions. Preflight opinions are labelled and cannot create entries.

The existing `gpt-6-luna` / low reasoning configuration is reused with the same 24KB input and 4096 output-token bounds. Each attempted model call reserves $0.025 using the existing conservative rate bounds. Both the recovery reviewer and this worker lock the same budget ledger before reserving, with a total $3 per actual IST day. Reservations are conservative accounting, not a bill or a cap on unrelated account usage/tax. Failed/uncertain calls retain reservations. Execution continues on open positions after the allowance is exhausted.

AWS storage: `/var/lib/nse-agent-trading` (agent.sqlite, independent strategy-lab.sqlite, atomic status/news snapshots), `/var/lib/nse-agent-budget/reviewer-budget.sqlite` (shared daily reservations). Migrate the old budget via SQLite backup with the equity recorder stopped; preserve history and the original file. Set protected `OPENAI_REVIEW_BUDGET_PATH=/budget/reviewer-budget.sqlite` and `OPENAI_REVIEW_DAILY_BUDGET_USD=3`, mount the shared directory into both consumers. Keep keys in protected reviewer.env, outside Git and images. Reports mount agent storage read-only. Systemd runs one worker under a host flock; it checks the existing session calendar internally.

The first deployment is prospective. No historical agent approvals are fabricated and ordinary strategy records are never rewritten. Evaluate recorded approved and rejected candidates across future sessions before claiming an improvement.

## Shadow decision outcomes

The worker records the first live WAIT or SKIP per technical candidate in `/agent/shadow/strategy-lab.sqlite`. This is a separate prospective opportunity ledger: repeated reviews of the same candidate do not create extra shadows. Each shadow keeps its original decision ID, reason and timestamp, and requires a strictly later fresh source trade within sixty seconds. Ten shares, the same price/extension bounds, 2R target, stop, 15:10 quote exit, fees and slippage are used. Genuine gaps stay unresolved. No-fill/expired candidates are neither wins nor avoided losses.

Shadows do not consume approved portfolio capacity or daily entry allowances; their sum is not an executable account return. A known closed positive shadow without a filled approval is a missed winner; a negative one is an avoided loss. Later filled approvals for the same candidate are linked and excluded from these counts, with approved-minus-shadow net shown only when both outcomes are closed and resolved. Each counterfactual remains tied to its decision time; a different technical candidate in the same stock can be a separate opportunity.

`/api/agent-trading` returns `shadow.rows`, action-specific summaries, coverage, no-fill counts and activation time. The Agent Trading page shows these alongside the approved portfolio. No additional model requests are made. An activation watermark prevents retroactive shadow entries from old decisions. After a restart, decisions recorded after activation are reconciled idempotently; an expired missed plan is marked no-fill rather than filled at an earlier price. Ordinary baseline and approved trade records are never rewritten.

### Session comparison and capital charts

`/comparison` automatically follows the latest three recorded sessions unless the URL pins `from` and `to`. The read-only `/api/comparison` aggregates the original live-paper ledgers, including the independent Agent Trading book. It excludes shadow trades and backtests. The leaderboard can sort by profitable complete sessions or known net, with daily coverage, costs, profit factor and complete-session endpoint drawdown. Missing outcomes never count as zero; dates without recorded trades cannot count as profitable sessions. Profit factor uses resolved closed trade net after the same saved/estimated charges as the overview. Different sizes and overlapping stocks mean this is not a capital-normalized ranking.

Agent Trading now includes full purchase-value exposure reconstructed from entry/exit timestamps, peak simultaneous exposure, outstanding exposure, cumulative buy/sell turnover, a peak-plus-known-session-cost buffer, and capital/realized-net charts. These are unleveraged cash exposure estimates, not broker margin calculations. Unknown release times retain exposure; missing costs are disclosed. Simultaneous releases and entries are netted at their recorded millisecond. The cost-buffer illustration does not guarantee sufficient funding for all possible losses or broker rules.

Click a stock in positions, decisions or shadows to inspect stored five-minute candles for the selected day. Actual entries/exits are drawn from the immutable quote ledger with stop and target levels; decision-only charts show the signal and supplied stop without inventing an entry/target. Missing candles remain blank. Candle versions are the latest persisted observations, not proof of what was available when a past decision was made. No additional provider calls, model reviews or trading actions are made by these pages.
