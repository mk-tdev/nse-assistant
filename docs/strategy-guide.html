Approximate paper model

Candidate with hypothetical fill

After 09:40, the completed next bar supplies its 09:35 opening price. This records a hypothetical fill, not an executed order.

Entry₹101.50
Stop₹96.00
2R target₹112.50

Risk per share = ₹101.50 − ₹96.00 = ₹5.50. Target = entry + 2 × risk.

Exact strategy: unverified in every scenario.
No timestamp-aligned exact VWAP input is implemented.

If the next open is at/below the stop, reject the entry. Stops can gap to a worse open; when stop and target are touched in the same bar, the simulator assumes the stop first. No position sizing is implemented. Fees, taxes and most slippage are excluded.

A useful morning is not a complete session

Morning analysis may still be possible

A 10:00 snapshot needs completed bars through 10:00, not future afternoon bars. Prior sessions missing only their tails may still supply the required morning volume slots.

Calendar, timestamp semantics, exact-slot history and freshness must still pass. Historical success does not prove live delivery is timely.

The unresolved 15:15 exit

Sampled one-minute data stops at 15:14. Its last five-minute aggregate starts at 15:10. Narrow requests for the missing tail return no rows.

A still-open paper trade cannot receive an invented 15:15 fill. Keep its outcome unresolved; a full-session analysis may become blocked.

14:15 · final 75 minutes of the session15:10 last observed start15:15 / 15:20 / 15:25 missing

In 144 sampled bars, start-aligned one-minute aggregation matched every five-minute OHLCV result; end-aligned aggregation matched none. This is empirical alignment evidence, not a current vendor timestamp guarantee. No verification flags were changed.

Prepare before 09:15

Verify session dates; resolve a small watchlist; load and validate the previous 20 same-slot volume observations. A missing or zero baseline blocks the candidate.

Capture and measure delivery

Fetch before the desired lower bound, then filter locally. Validate live REST completeness. A future WebSocket recorder could capture the session tail, but it needs clock, continuity, volume and reconnect verification. It cannot repair missing history by assumption.

Evaluate only completed candles

Recheck freshness at each five-minute completion. Missing latest bars mean stale/blocked. Record an approximate candidate separately from exact qualification; deduplicate repeated checks.

Preserve outcomes and uncertainty

Persist paper events. At 15:15 use an observed source; the current bar simulator waits until 15:20. If data is absent, leave the exit unresolved. Do not shorten the session to fit the returned data.
