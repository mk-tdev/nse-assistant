How to read this comparison

Profitable-session counts and daily averages use fully resolved, closed sessions with entered trades. No recorded trades, partial outcomes and open positions are separate states. No recorded trades can mean the strategy was inactive or had no eligible setup; it does not prove a signal was rejected.

Known net deducts all known fees, taxes and modeled slippage. Missing outcomes are excluded, never replaced by zero. Profit factor uses resolved closed trade net gains / absolute net losses. Drawdown is measured at complete session endpoints; it is not intraday drawdown and is partial if any active session is incomplete.

Agent Trading is a separate row with its actual recorded history. Shadow opportunities and historical simulations are excluded. Strategies with duplicate trades are not independent confirmation. Cloud, AI and data subscription costs are excluded.

Results refresh automatically every 60 seconds. Date selections are shareable in the URL. Leave dates unpinned to follow the latest three recorded sessions.
